## What the release decided

- Research has its own export chooser. Opening Research never downloads a map.
- Each export's analysis availability is read from its own interp index.
  Nothing claims all 26 analyses.
- The Research bundle is SHA-256-verified before its chart renders. A missing
  or altered bundle shows recovery instead.
- Without a GPU, Research shows the method, source and table in place of the
  chart, and the task still completes.
- An analysis requested by a link or by Methods survives the export choice.
- Pinned findings and links name an immutable artifact by digest, and the
  host keeps every published digest.
- `experience.json` and `index.json` revalidate on every boot.
- Settings ownership per experience is in `docs/SETTINGS-OWNERSHIP.md`.

## Open items

These are owner decisions or work that needs people or hardware.

- **N09 audience evaluation.** It needs 15 participants: five newcomers in
  Learn, five builders in Atlas and five researchers in Research. The target
  is four of five unaided in each cohort. Until then every experience is beta.
- **N10 recordings and case study.** These wait on N09 evidence, per the plan.
- **Caddy cache headers.** `out/` is served `public, max-age=3600`. Marking
  `out/artifacts/` immutable is optional and owner-edited.
- **PsychiX hub links.** The branded shell should link the three entries with
  their descriptions. It is deployed separately.
- **gpt2 sidecars.** `channels.json` and `directions.json` exist only on the
  build machine, which is why live shows 24 of 26 analyses.
- **Steer rail.** No intervention sweeps are published, so it stays unavailable.
- **`compare/metrics.json`.** The live copy is kept on purpose. Replacing it is
  the owner's call.
- **Old hashed chunks.** They accumulate in `assets/`; prune by hand (§10.3 of
  `DEPLOY-STATIC.md`).
